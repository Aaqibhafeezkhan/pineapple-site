    What it can do

    Browse deeplyArtist pages (releases, singles, videos, related, bios) and album pages (cover, year, tracks).
    Instant searchSongs, artists, albums — debounced, cancel-safe, no network request per keystroke.
    Reach the playerMini-player everywhere; full-player with art, scrubber, queue sheet; notification controls & lockscreen.
    Forgives bad networksOffline states, reconnect banner, cached recents, retry that doesn't silently refetch.
    Library on-deviceLikes, recents, search history live in the app storage — no signup, no cloud.
    Token economyZero icon libraries (bundled vectors only). No ads. No telemetry. Android 8.0+.
